SiteMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

SiteMind is a WhatsApp assistant for construction project teams. People whose numbers have been added to a project can ask questions and get answers from that project's documents. SiteMind is currently a pilot in testing.

Information we collect

For phone numbers added to a project, we store:

  • the phone number, and any name and project role entered for it
  • the text of messages sent to SiteMind, and the replies it sends
  • the type of any other message, such as "image" or "audio", but not the file itself
  • the date and time of each message, and the sources cited in each reply

We also store the project documents that are added to SiteMind.

If a number hasn't been added to a project, we don't reply to it and we don't store its messages.

How we use information

We use this information to answer questions, check that answers match their sources, and improve the service. We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Services that process information

  • Meta (WhatsApp) delivers messages.
  • Supabase hosts the database and application.
  • Voyage AI processes questions and document text to make them searchable.
  • Groq processes questions and relevant document text to write answers.

These companies may process information outside your country.

How long we keep information

We keep information until it's no longer needed for the project, or until you ask us to delete it. Information isn't deleted automatically at this stage.

Deleting your information

To ask us to delete your phone number and messages, email sanjula2301@gmail.com from any address and include the phone number. Messages already delivered to your WhatsApp stay on your device, and you can delete them there.
